What is here today.

Each package is a crates.io package adopted as a Loaf once, with its source verified against the crates.io checksum and its build facts recorded, so a build script never has to run on your machine. A compiled unit is published only when two independent builds come out byte-identical, and Oven takes one only when its identity equals what your build needs. Otherwise it bakes from source.

Owner publishing isn’t open yet, and the registry’s records aren’t signed yet. Every digest can be checked against the published index.
